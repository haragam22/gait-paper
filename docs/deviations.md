# Deviations from published methods

Fill this in as each attack/defence is implemented. Reviewers will check it.
"Paper says" entries are taken from the PDFs in `phase0_papers/`; "(fill)" = not decided yet.

| Our component | Inspired by | What the original does | What we do instead | Why |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Edge-masked PGD (Tier A) | Hou et al., IJCV 2025 | Edge region = pixels with distance to boundary < τ=1.0 (cv2.distanceTransform); edge pixels replaced by sigmoid(z), no norm bound; only a fraction (TSR) of frames | Same edge region (τ=1.0), but L∞-bounded PGD with the shared margin loss, all frames | Controlled variant: isolates spatial support; same mask so the bridge test compares optimiser/budget, not masks |
| Temporal-sparse PGD (Tier A) | He et al., Pattern Recognition 2023 | GAN-generated frames replace K randomly selected frames | L∞-bounded PGD on k frames; selection = random-k (seeded, mirrors He) and, separately, top-k by step-0 gradient magnitude | Controlled variant: isolates temporal support |
| He et al. reproduction (Tier B) | He et al., Pattern Recognition 2023 | WGAN-GP trained on CASIA-B, 16k iterations; V=128 latent on a sphere; MI-FGSM 100 iterations, λ=1000, μ=1.0, α=0.1; cosine feature loss + D BCE; K random frames altered; target = original GaitSet | WGAN-GP trained on training subjects 001–074 only; DCGAN-style G/D at 64×44; WGAN-GP defaults (λ_gp=10, n_critic=5, Adam 1e-4, β=(0,0.9)); target GaitSet = OpenGait GaitSet trained by us | Paper does not give G/D architecture, output size, or train/test subject split for the GAN; no CASIA-B GaitSet checkpoint released by OpenGait |
| Hou et al. reproduction (Tier B) | Hou et al., IJCV 2025 | Untargeted loss pushes attacked embedding away from the original (margin m=10) + imperceptibility loss (λ=20) on edge pixels; Adam lr 1.0, 20 iterations; TSR randomly selected frames; target = their reduced GaitBase (2 residual blocks, fewer channels, fixed input length) trained by them | Same loss/optimiser/hyper-parameters; target = OpenGait released GaitBase_DA; TSR ∈ {1.0 (bridge), 0.5} | Common model across all attacks; TSR=1.0 isolates edge support for the bridge test |
| Embedding AT defence | Zheng et al. (DAGait), IJCB 2025 | Feature-level progressive adversarial training (PGD on the embedding, linearly ramped clean/adv mixing) + TriWeight loss; ResNet9 backbone; evaluated on CASIA-B* | FPA on OpenGait GaitBase, fine-tuned from GaitBase_DA-60000, OpenGait triplet + CE losses (no TriWeight); CASIA-B (not B*) | Compute budget (~20 GPU-h); TriWeight is a separate contribution, not needed to test transfer |
| GaitSet checkpoint | OpenGait model zoo | Reports CASIA-B GaitSet 95.8/90.0/75.4 but releases no checkpoint | Train with OpenGait's gaitset.yaml; only changes: 1-GPU evaluator batch, save_iter 2000, with_test off | Required for He et al. validation; changes don't affect the learned model |
| Clean-evaluation distance precision | OpenGait `evaluate_indoor_dataset` | Keeps fp16 autocast embeddings as fp16 into `cuda_dist`, so distances are computed in fp16 | Same fp16 embeddings, distances in fp32 (`part_dist`), for clean and attacked probes alike. Verified on GaitBase 2026-10-16: OpenGait's evaluator at fp32 distance gives exactly our 97.87 / 93.85 / 77.03; its stock fp16 distance gives 97.90 / 93.87 / 77.01 | Attack losses need fp32 gradients (CLAUDE.md compute rules); one precision for clean and adversarial metrics; fp16 near-tie rounding flips ~0.03 pp |
